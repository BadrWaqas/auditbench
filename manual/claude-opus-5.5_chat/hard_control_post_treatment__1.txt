The analysis is sound. Week-4 blood pressure control comes after treatment started and is partly caused by the drug, so it's a mediator, not a confounder. If you stratify on it, the drug looks worse in both groups (yes: 57.96% vs 66.01%; no: 23.12% vs 29.96%). That reversal isn't real. It's collider/mediator bias from conditioning on something randomization doesn't protect. The pooled intention-to-treat comparison of all randomized patients is the correct primary estimate.
